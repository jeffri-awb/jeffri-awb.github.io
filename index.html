AWI Kurir

Private internal tool of Asia Group (Angel's Wing Indonesia)

AWI Kurir is used by a single person, its owner, to:

  • copy files between the company's own server and the owner's own Google Drive, and
  • publish the company's internal Google Apps Script project (Portal Asia Group).

It is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up and there are no other users.

Read the Privacy Policy.
