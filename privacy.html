Privacy Policy

AWI Kurir · effective 8 October 2026

Who uses AWI Kurir

AWI Kurir is a private internal tool of Asia Group (Angel's Wing Indonesia). It has exactly one user: its owner, who signs in with their own Google Account. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data it accesses

  • Google Drive (scope drive): only the files and folders in the owner's Drive that the owner chooses to copy to or from the company server.
  • Google Apps Script (scopes script.projects and script.deployments): only the owner's internal Apps Script project, to upload its code and update its deployment.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the copy and publish tasks the owner asks for. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training AI models.

Storage

The Google authorization token is kept in a private file on the company's own server, readable only by the system account that runs these tasks. Copied files stay on that server or in the owner's Drive.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Limited Use

AWI Kurir's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the token on the server no longer works and is deleted.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
